CASE 001: THE SILENT ROOM

Victim: Mr. Adrian

Location: Locked study room

Incident: Found dead at 9:00 PM.

Suspects:

  • John – His business partner
  • Mary – His wife
  • David – The housekeeper

Clues:

  • A wet umbrella was found inside the room.
  • The window was locked from inside.
  • John claims he left at 8:30 PM.
  • Mary says she heard a clock stop at 8:45 PM.
  • David says it did not rain that evening.

Who is lying? Who is the killer?

CASE 001 – SOLUTION

Killer: John.

The wet umbrella suggests someone entered from outside during rain. David claims there was no rain, while John's departure story needs further investigation. The clues are not sufficient to prove John's guilt conclusively. This case is deliberately open to investigation.
